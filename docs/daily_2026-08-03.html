cs.AI

CAGE: Certified Authorization under Typed-Return Uncertainty for Tool-Using Agents ⭐ 精选
Blaise Delattre, Cong Wang, Yang Cao
cs.AI
Tool-using LLM agents act on typed tool returns where small binding faults or numerical drift can flip an authorized action into an unsafe one. The authors show that certifying the categorical and numerical channels separately is not compositional: per-channel safe perturbations can jointly become unsafe. CAGE directly certifies the joint neighborhood by enumerating discrete binding branches and bounding continuous drift within each branch, offering CAGE-Exact for executable policies and CAGE-Lip/CAGE-RS for learned gates under fidelity assumptions. In synthetic, policy-as-code, regulatory, and real-transaction tests, CAGE eliminates in-budget false allows while retaining a useful fraction of autonomous decisions.

Tool Specifications Matter: Uncovering and Mitigating Safety Risks in AI Agents ⭐ 精选
Minghui Pan et al.
cs.AI

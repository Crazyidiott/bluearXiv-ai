KernelGenBench: A Multi-Source and Multi-Chip Benchmark for LLM-based Kernel Generation ⭐ 精选
Peiyu Zang et al.
cs.AI cs.LG
针对LLM和agent自动生成加速器kernel的研究，现有工作缺乏覆盖多算子来源与异构硬件的统一评测基准，难以可靠评估生成结果的可移植性与实际效果。KernelGenBench构建了双子基准：KernelGenBench-MS包含210个来自cuBLAS等非PyTorch标准源的算子，KernelGenBench-MC在六种异构硬件上评测110个算子的性能可移植性。大规模评测显示，基于agent的方法整体优于纯LLM采样，但跨平台性能退化严重，且agent方法生成单个成功算子平均消耗约5.11 million tokens，成本远高于简单采样方法。

Rehearse: Stepping Back from the Confidence Cliff in Self-Improving Autoresearch ⭐ 精选
Jiazhen Ji, Shouhong Ding
cs.AI
Autoresearch loops rely on an agent's pre-execution judgment to filter candidate modifications, yet this reliability deteriorates as successful changes accumulate, a pattern termed the confidence cliff: selective accuracy drops from 82.8% to 56.9% while the judge remains decisive. The authors propose Rehearse, a lightweight skill that proposes multiple ideas, compares them before execution, and judges using a focused memory of similar past attempts and outcomes. This design restores late selective accuracy to 83.5% and improves the endpoint metric under the same training-run budget across nanochat, image classification, and time-series forecasting. The work highlights the need for reliability-aware loop design in self-improving agentic systems.

Share the Judge, Learn the Deferral: Where Specialization Helps LLM Evaluation ⭐ 精选
Weining Zhang
cs.AI
该论文研究LLM评估中领域专业化应放在evaluator权重还是deferral规则中的架构问题，基于99,952个rubric-conditioned样本与RewardBench 2实验。结果显示，将训练语料按criterion族拆分为8个LoRA judge会损失10.05点accuracy，并令5%风险目标下的audited coverage从24.44%降至5.43%；而从共享trained judge初始化family adapters可恢复至76.85% accuracy，比scratch training高19.94点。相反，若将专业化用于控制deferral（如通过learned correctness heads在0.6B-4B-8B cascade中路由样本而不改变reward score），则可在0.415 normalized parameter compute下达到89.40% accuracy，优于单独8B的84.75%，且所有run通过exact one-sided 95% risk audit。结论是应共享judgment learning直到有足够数据再拆分，并将domain-specific adaptation置于可审计的release boundary中。

cs.AI cs.LG

cs.AI

SciDataSailor: Deep Scientific Data Exploring ⭐ 精选
Jiyong Rao et al.
cs.AI cs.CL
科学数据常以层级化仓库形式组织，内部文件异构且相互依赖，传统检查与整合依赖大量领域专家，而现有LLM agent研究缺乏在可执行环境中操作真实数据资产的能力。作者提出Deep Scientific Data Exploration任务范式，并构建SciDataSailor框架，将轨迹合成建模为Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)，引入难度分层种子、双反馈首次探索紧迫性、层级策略到工具动作生成以及熵引导分支等机制，以平衡全局探索与定向利用。基于该框架生成SciDataSailor-SFT-2K用于监督微调，并构建包含627个元信息总结与586个科学问答任务的SciDataSailor-Bench评测基准。实验表明该agent框架能有效提升模型在跨文件证据整合与可执行观测结论生成上的可靠性。
MIND: Lightweight and Effective Memory Injection Defense for LLM Agents via Intent-Aware Information Bottleneck ⭐ 精选
Dongyi Liu et al.

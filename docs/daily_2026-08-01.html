Rehearse: Stepping Back from the Confidence Cliff in Self-Improving Autoresearch ⭐ 精选
Jiazhen Ji, Shouhong Ding
cs.AI
Autoresearch loops face a confidence cliff: as successful modifications accumulate, the agent's pre-execution judgment on proposed changes degrades sharply, with selective accuracy falling from 82.8% to 56.9% while the judge remains overconfident. To address this, Rehearse introduces a lightweight skill that proposes multiple candidate ideas, compares them before running training, and evaluates the most promising one using a focused memory of similar past attempts and outcomes. This targeted outcome memory restores late selective accuracy to 83.5%, and under a fixed budget of 4,000 training runs across three loops, Rehearse improves endpoint performance on nanochat, image classification, and time-series forecasting tasks.
